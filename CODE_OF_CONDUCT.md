# Code of Conduct

This code applies to everyone who takes part in this project: in issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, documentation, assessments and events connected with it.

Adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1, licensed under CC BY 4.0 (https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). The wording here has been changed for this project. The adaptation is not endorsed by the Contributor Covenant authors.

## Our commitment

We want participation in this project to be free of harassment for everyone, whatever their age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, experience level, nationality, appearance, race, religion or sexual orientation, and whatever their role.

We ask contributors to keep discussion focused on the protocol, the evidence and the evaluator. Criticism of a claim, a design or a result is welcome. Criticism of a person is not.

## Standards

Behaviour that helps the project includes:

- giving specific, reproducible reasons for a technical objection;
- crediting others' work and stating what you did not verify;
- accepting correction of your own claims;
- respecting the limits the project sets, such as the rules on private data and on what may be claimed as conformance.

Behaviour that is not acceptable includes:

- harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks;
- sexualised content, or unwelcome sexual attention;
- publishing another person's private information without consent, including records, credentials or private traces;
- deliberately misrepresenting a result, a conformance status or an affiliation;
- sustained disruption of discussion, including bad-faith reports or repeated objections without new reasons.

## Responsibilities

Maintainers explain which behaviour is unacceptable and may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that break this code. They may temporarily or permanently restrict a contributor's participation and say why.

## Scope

This code applies within project spaces and when a person represents the project in public. It also applies to private communication about the project where the matter concerns project participation.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the enforcement contact below. Include what happened, where and when, and any evidence you hold. Do not include credentials or personal data beyond what is needed. Security defects are reported through `SECURITY.md`, not through this channel.

Enforcement contact: [ENFORCEMENT CONTACT: the repository owner must set a monitored channel before this code is adopted or relied on. No address has been provided and none has been invented.]

Reports are handled confidentially, as far as the process allows. No response time is promised until the owner sets a monitored channel.

## Enforcement

Maintainers apply these steps in proportion to the breach:

1. **Correction.** A private note explaining the issue and what is expected.
2. **Warning.** A formal warning, with consequences for further breach.
3. **Temporary restriction.** Limited participation for a stated period.
4. **Permanent restriction.** Removal from project spaces.

Maintainers who are involved in a report do not decide it. A person who is the subject of a report may give their account in writing before a decision is taken.

## Appeals

A person sanctioned under this code may ask for a review by a maintainer who was not involved. The review is recorded. The project has no independent panel yet (see `docs/governance/DECISION-PROCESS.md`).
