# Crosswalk: EU AI Act high-risk obligations

Scope: Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, Articles 9 (risk management), 12 (record-keeping), 14 (human oversight) and 15 (accuracy, robustness and cybersecurity). Article numbers and titles were read from the EUR-Lex page of the Regulation, fetched through a tool that returns a condensed view of the page, so paragraph topics are paraphrases and must be checked against the Official Journal text before any quotation. Whether a given agent is a high-risk AI system, who is provider or deployer, and the application dates (including any amendment after this draft) were NOT checked. An AI Act conformity assessment is a legal process; a KYA assessment is not one and does not replace it.

Source: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=OJ:L_202401689

Status meanings: `supports as input` means a KYA artifact can be one input to the evidence a practitioner assembles for the clause; `partial` means it addresses one aspect only; `not addressed` means KYA has no artifact for it. None of these states means the clause is met.
