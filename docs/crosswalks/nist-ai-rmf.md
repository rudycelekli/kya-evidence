# Crosswalk: NIST AI RMF 1.0 (Govern, Map, Measure, Manage)

Scope: selected subcategories of NIST AI 100-1 (AI RMF 1.0), Tables 1 to 4 of the core. The PDF was fetched in full and the subcategory identifiers and statements were read from the extracted text. The framework is voluntary. The AI RMF Playbook, the Generative AI Profile and any later revision were NOT read. Subcategories not listed here were not mapped, which is not a statement that KYA has nothing for them.

Source: https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ai/NIST.AI.100-1.pdf

Status meanings: `supports as input` means a KYA artifact can be one input to the evidence a practitioner assembles for the clause; `partial` means it addresses one aspect only; `not addressed` means KYA has no artifact for it. None of these states means the clause is met.
