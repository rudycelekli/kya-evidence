# KYA Evidence

## Register

brand

## Users

Developers, relying parties and independent assessors inspect an exact agent configuration, understand what supplied evidence supports, and reproduce a decision. The README reader moves between a repository and a terminal. The report reader examines failures, unknowns and evidence boundaries before acting.

## Product Purpose

The implemented package pairs a manifest and relying policy with signed receipts, frozen checks, reproducible research snapshots, structured-response diagnostic dossiers and offline evaluator-control benchmarks. Its ambition is a neutral, contestable Know Your Agent standard. Current source delivers an experimental local reference implementation; external adoption and predictive calibration remain unestablished.

## Brand Personality

Precise, candid, ambitious. Confidence comes from reproducible evidence and visible limits. Documentation presents capabilities directly, names the observation tier and makes a failed or missing measurement readable.

## Anti-references

Avoid invented adopter logos, official-certification badges, live product rankings from synthetic data, decorative trust seals and unexplained single-number reputation. Avoid generic dashboard card grids, neon security theater, glass decoration and animation that hides unknown evidence.

## Design Principles

Show the evidence path and preserve its branching limits. Keep scope and source visible beside every result. Keep failed and unmeasured checks in the population. Make exact reproduction a short path from the README. Make the distinction between signed assertions, replayed responses and independently observed effects understandable.

## Accessibility and Inclusion

Readable scalable vector labels, title/description metadata, color-redundant statuses, reduced-motion fallback, offline assets and print-friendly reports. Source tests verify these controls; no browser visual or physical printing validation is asserted.
