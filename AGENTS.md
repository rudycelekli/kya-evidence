# KYA Evidence development contract

This is a clean-room, Apache-2.0 implementation. Do not copy private Gradia code, client data, credentials, internal paths or operational logs into distributable files.

Bind every assessment to exact manifest, suite, evaluator, policy and evidence digests. Signature validity proves integrity and possession of a signing key; it does not prove truthful execution, human identity, legal authority or independent review.

Keep declared, fixture-tested, observed-runtime and independently assessed evidence distinct. Fixture demonstrations never score commercial products. Unknown checks remain unknown; do not remove them from the denominator. Hard failures cannot be averaged away. Assessment is not an authorization grant or a credit score for a person.

Changes to protocol or evaluator require a substantive ADR, adversarial regression and a test that detects the weakened gate. Compare baseline and candidate on identical frozen cases. A candidate cannot edit its evaluator or expected outputs. Preserve raw evidence privately; publish only authorized sanitized artifacts.

Execute native subscription agents in isolated, owned lanes. No separately billed model calls, public publication, deployment, merges, destructive operations, auto-updates or external messages without human authorization. Reversible local changes and local tests are preauthorized. Existing upstream projects are read-only inputs.

Use SPARC. Record task owners, inputs, versions, decisions, failures, costs (null if unmeasured), tests and next actions in local evidence. ADR statuses reflect implementation and review evidence; do not claim standards-body acceptance.
